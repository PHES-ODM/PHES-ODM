# Acknowledgements 

This example data was generated from the published data in PHES-ODM version 1.0 or 1.1 from from the following sources:

- The Quebec City wastewater surveillance work, available through their [Zenodo repository](https://zenodo.org/records/5597158).
- The Ontario Wastewater Surveillance Consortium [available through their GitHub page](https://github.com/OntarioWastewaterSurveillanceConsortium/sars-cov-2-data).
- Some additional data also comes from the [Delatolla Lab](https://www.delatolla-research-group.ca), who are members of the Ontario Wastewater Surveillance Consortium, but also have made additional data public at the [PHESD repository](https://github.com/Delatolla-lab/PHESD), in partnership with Ottawa Public Health. 

These citations are also present in the data through the existing `databases` and `organizations` tables in version 3 of the PHES-ODM.
